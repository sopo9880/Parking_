Parking Research Agent v16.5.0
================================

Role
----
Validation/infrastructure release. v16.2 remains the protected SAFE_BASELINE and v16.4 remains a CANDIDATE until independent evidence supports promotion.

Validation Center
-----------------
- Local Dataset Profiles for video/GT/camera-count/cut/repeat settings.
- Configurable 1-12 CCTV views instead of assuming exactly three.
- Dynamic ROI setup, preview, duplicate linker, diagnostics and paper screenshots.
- Dynamic count-GT template generator and interactive 10-second GT labeler.

Episode / cut-boundary evaluation
---------------------------------
- Cut boundaries such as 4:00, 8:00, 12:00, 16:00.
- Post-cut warm-up mask so artificial clip jumps are excluded from episode metrics.
- episode_evaluation_mask.csv and episode_metrics.csv.

Repeated-source stability
-------------------------
- repeat_period_sec / repeat_count settings.
- Compares each repeated segment against repeat 1.
- Reports agreement, MAE-vs-first and max drift.
- Explicitly a stability/reproducibility stress test, NOT independent validation.

v16.4 automatic evaluation
---------------------------
- ALL-IN-ONE automatically runs the v16.4 transition refiner after v16.2 SAFE_BASELINE.
- Candidate metrics, transition audit and event-balanced metrics are packaged in UPLOAD_TO_CHATGPT.zip.
- No automatic SAFE promotion.

Public external validation
--------------------------
- Automatic MetaPKLot / CNRPark-EXT preparation.
- Quick and Standard modes download selected official images plus matching spot annotations.
- Full mode is explicit opt-in and shallow-clones the upstream repository.
- Resume + size verification for targeted downloads.
- COCO-style parking-space annotations are converted to external_gt_spots.csv.
- Reports Accuracy, Precision, F1, Occupied Recall, Empty Specificity and TP/TN/FP/FN, including camera/weather breakdown.
- Builds EXTERNAL_VALIDATION_TO_CHATGPT.zip.
- Public results are external spatial generalization, not continuous temporal-transition validation.

Cache visibility
----------------
- Main UI shows a compact CACHE audit summary.
- Existing detector/slot/evidence/SEG/robustness cache separation remains intact.

Data/update safety
------------------
- user_data/ remains update-preserved and stores profiles/public datasets.
- Existing settings/slots/ROI/output/work/.venv preservation remains unchanged.
